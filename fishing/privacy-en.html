Midday Catch Privacy Policy

  • App Name: Midday Catch (Google Play Package: com.sbgc.fishing)
  • Data Controller: Individual Developer Kim Seunghoon (Google Play Developer Name: s.h.)
  • Effective Date: November 3, 2026
  • Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Kim Seunghoon (the "Developer") processes the personal information of Midday Catch users (the "App") in accordance with applicable laws including the Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the App collects, where it is stored, who receives it, and how users can exercise their rights.

Summary

  • The App has no user registration and the Developer does not operate its own server or use analytics tools.
  • Game records and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Information necessary for Play Games (leaderboard, achievements), Google Play billing, and AdMob ads is sent to Google LLC. Logging in to Play Games and making purchases are optional.
  • The App displays AdMob ads. In the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, whether personalized ads are shown depends on your consent choice. In other regions, only non-personalized ads are requested.

1.5 Google Play App Quality Information (Android vitals)

Google Play collects quality information like app crashes and unresponsive states from devices of users with "Usage and diagnostics data" enabled and provides it to the Developer through Google Play Console (Android vitals). This information is collected by Google at the device level, not by the app, and collection depends on user device settings. The Developer uses this information only for bug fixes and quality improvement.

Reference: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486

2. Ads and Your Choices

The App displays AdMob ads (3 rewarded ad units, 1 interstitial ad unit).

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: A Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen is displayed and personalized ads are determined by your choice. Ads are not requested before the consent process is complete. You can change your choice anytime in [Settings] > [Privacy & Ad Choices] (this menu only appears in regions where consent is required).
  • Other regions (including Korea): The App requests only non-personalized ads. There is no menu in the app to enable personalized ads.
  • Even non-personalized ads may use cookies or advertising IDs for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting. Reference: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7676680
  • You can delete or reset your advertising ID in device settings.
  • If you purchase ad removal, both rewarded and interstitial ads will not be displayed.

3. Retention Period and Deletion

  • Information saved on device: Deleted immediately when the app is uninstalled or when storage data is cleared in device app settings.
  • Android Auto Backup: App data is not included in Android Auto Backup.
  • Information sent to Google: Retained by Google according to Google's privacy policy. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Inquiry emails: Deleted promptly from inbox and trash after processing. If retention is required by law, relevant information is separately stored for the required period.

5. Security Measures

As an individual developer, the Developer implements the following measures:

  • No own server or own accounts, so the Developer does not collect or store user personal information centrally.
  • Game data is stored in Android's app-exclusive storage area that blocks access from other apps. App data is not included in Android Auto Backup.
  • Location, contacts, storage, camera, and microphone permissions are not requested. Permissions used by the App are vibration, internet, network status check, Google Play Billing, advertising ID, Android Ad Services (advertising ID, ad contribution measurement, topics of interest), device wake lock, foreground services, and app-specific permissions to block internal signals from other apps. Except for vibration, these are permissions added by Google Play Billing, ads, Play Games SDK, and Android libraries. None require separate user consent.
  • Payment method information is not received.
  • Inquiry emails are accessed only by the Data Protection Officer.
  • Google publicly states that Play Games Service and Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypt collected data during transmission.
  • If a personal information breach is discovered, the Developer notifies users as required by law and reports to the Personal Information Protection Commission or Korea Internet & Security Agency if necessary.
