  • App name: 윷 데일리 챌린지 (Yut Daily Challenge) (Google Play package name: com.sbgc.yut)
  • Controller: Kim Seunghoon, individual developer (Google Play developer name: s.h.)

  • Effective date: November 3, 2026

Kim Seunghoon (the "developer") handles the personal information of people who use Yut Daily Challenge (the "app") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the app stores, where it is stored, to whom it is sent, and how you can make choices and exercise your rights. This is a translation of the Korean policy. The app itself is available in Korean only. If the two versions differ, the Korean version applies, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

Summary

  • The app has no sign-up. The developer runs no servers of its own and uses no analytics tools.
  • Game records, settings, and names entered in Play Together are stored only on your device and are not sent to the developer.
  • When you use ads (Google AdMob) or Google Play billing, the information they need is sent to Google LLC.
  • The current version does not connect to Google Play Games (leaderboards and achievements). The Play Games items in this policy apply from the update that connects Play Games, and we will announce that on this page in advance. Even after it is connected, you can use the whole game without signing in to Play Games.
  • Depending on your region, you can refuse personalized ads or change your consent inside the app.

1.4 Information the developer does not collect

  • We do not ask for personal details such as phone number, address, or date of birth. Play Together names are stored only on the device, as described in 1.1.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, photos/storage, camera, or microphone permissions.
  • The App does not use its own crash reporting or analytics tools. However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may send error details to Google when an error occurs in the ad feature; this information is described in section 1.2. Information collected by the Play Games SDK on its own, once connected, is also described in section 1.2.

1.5 App quality information from Google Play (Android vitals)

On some devices whose users have turned on "automatically share usage and diagnostics," Google Play collects quality information such as app crashes and freezes and provides it to the developer through Google Play Console (Android vitals). Google collects this at the device level, not the app, and whether it is collected depends on your device settings. The developer uses this information only to fix errors and improve quality.
Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486

1.6 Closed test builds

Closed test builds have a [Copy test statistics] item on the [About] screen. Tapping it only copies app usage statistics such as number of games, win rate, and screen size to the device clipboard; nothing is sent automatically. The statistics contain no account, names (including Play Together names), advertising ID, or device identifiers. The developer receives them only if a tester pastes and sends them, and uses them only to analyze test results. Public release builds do not have this feature.

2. Ads and your choices

The app shows ads so that it can be offered for free. The app does not start the ad SDK until it has checked with Google UMP whether consent is needed in your region. The in-app ad choice item is at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Ad privacy choices] (in the Korean app: [설정] > [개인정보·계정] > [광고 개인정보 선택]). As described below, it is shown or hidden depending on your region.

2.1 Users in Korea

  • On first launch, the [Ad notice] screen asks whether you consent to personalized ads. You can choose [I am 14 or older, I agree] ([만 14세 이상, 동의]) or [I do not agree] ([동의하지 않음]).
  • If you choose [I do not agree] or do not answer, only non-personalized ads are requested. This makes no difference to gameplay.
  • You can choose again at any time at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Ad privacy choices].
  • Only users aged 14 or older may choose [I am 14 or older, I agree]. The app has no process to obtain and verify a legal guardian's consent, so if you are under 14, please choose [I do not agree]. If you have already agreed, you can change it in [Ad privacy choices].
  • Even on a device judged to be in Korea, if Google judges that consent is required under the rules of the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, Google's consent screen in 2.2 appears instead of the [Ad notice], and only non-personalized ads are requested regardless of your choice.

2.2 Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland

  • Google's consent screen appears on first launch.
  • You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Ad privacy choices].

2.3 Residents of certain US states

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Ad privacy choices].

If you opt out, your choice is passed to Google, data processing is restricted, and personalized ads are not shown. You can change this choice at any time in the same menu. The developer does not sell personal information for money.

2.4 Users in other regions

The in-app ad choice item is not shown. You can manage ads in these ways:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in the ads menu of your device settings (the menu location depends on the device and Android version)
  • Google My Ad Center: https://myadcenter.google.com

2.5 Automatic collection

The app itself uses no cookies or tracking tools. The ad SDK collects the information in 1.2, and you can refuse personalized ads by the methods in 2.1 to 2.4. If you do not want the information needed to show ads to be sent at all, please stop using and delete the app.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Inquiry emails: deleted from the mailbox and trash without delay once handled. If the law requires us to keep them, we keep the basis and items separately and delete them when the period ends.

5. Security measures

As an individual developer, the developer takes these measures:

  • With no servers or accounts of its own, the developer does not gather or store users' personal information.
  • Game data and Play Together names are stored in the app-private storage area that Android protects from other apps, and are excluded from Android backup.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone permissions. The permissions it uses are internet, network state, vibration, Google Play billing, and the advertising ID, ad measurement, and background work permissions used by the ad SDK. All are normal permissions that are not separately requested from you.
  • The developer does not receive payment details.
  • Only the privacy officer reads inquiry emails.
  • Google states that data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit with TLS and data collected by Play Games Services (once connected) is encrypted in transit with HTTPS.
  • If the developer learns of a personal information breach, it will notify users as required by law and, where necessary, report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission or the Korea Internet & Security Agency.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You (or a legal guardian for a child under 14) may request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information, and may withdraw consent. The only personal information the developer keeps directly is inquiry email, so you can exercise most rights yourself as follows:

  • Delete device data: delete the app or clear its storage data in your device's app info settings.
  • Play Together names: change them or reset them to defaults at any time on the Play Together setup screen.
  • Change or withdraw personalized ad consent: follow Section 2.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in the ads menu of your device settings.
  • Play Games (not connected in the current version; applies from the connecting update): signing in is optional, and you can sign in with the Play Games sign-in button in the app (for example, on the Today's Yut result screen). If your device has a Play Games profile, you may be signed in automatically when the app starts (1.2). The app has no sign-out menu. You can delete this game's Play Games data and profile in your Google Play Games profile settings: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
  • Purchase history: check your Google Play purchase history in your Google account. You can reload purchase status at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Restore purchases] ([구매 복원]).
  • Other requests: email us at the address in Section 9. We reply within 10 days of receiving your request and, if we cannot handle it in that time, we will tell you why and when we expect to. We may ask for additional information only as needed to confirm that the request comes from you.

7. Children's personal information

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information. In Korea, if consent is needed to process the personal information of a child under 14, a legal guardian's consent must be obtained. The app has no such process, so it does not ask users under 14 to consent to personalized ads (2.1). If you believe a child's personal information is being processed, please let us know at the email address in Section 9.
