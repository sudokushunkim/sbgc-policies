Ur Daily Challenge Privacy Policy

  • App name: Ur Daily Challenge (Google Play package name: com.sbgc.ur)
  • Data controller: Kim Seunghoon, individual developer (Google Play developer name: s.h.)
  • Effective date: November 30, 2026
  • Last updated: October 8, 2026

Kim Seunghoon (the "developer") processes the personal information of people who use Ur Daily Challenge (the "app") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the app stores, where it goes, and how you can make choices and exercise your rights.

Summary

  • The app has no sign-up. The developer runs no servers of its own and uses no analytics tools.
  • Your game records and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to the developer.
  • When you see ads (Google AdMob), use the optional Play Games sign-in, or pay through Google Play, the information needed for that feature is sent to Google LLC.
  • Depending on your region, you can refuse or change your consent to personalized ads inside the app.

1.4 Information we do not collect

1.5 App quality data from Google Play (Android vitals)

Google Play collects quality data, such as app crashes and "app not responding" events, from a subset of devices whose users have turned on automatic sharing of usage and diagnostics data, and gives it to the developer through Google Play Console (Android vitals). Google collects this at the device level, not through the app, and whether it is collected depends on your device settings. The developer uses it only to fix errors and improve quality.
Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486

1.6 Closed test builds

In closed test builds, the [About] screen has a [Copy test stats] item. Tapping it copies app usage statistics, such as games played, win rates and screen size, to your device clipboard. Nothing is sent automatically. The statistics contain no account, name, advertising ID or device identifier. The developer receives them only if a tester pastes and sends them, and uses them only to analyze test results. The public release build does not have this feature.

2. Ads and your choices

The app shows ads so it can be offered for free. The app does not start the ad SDK until it has checked with Google UMP whether consent is required in your region. The in-app ad choice item is at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Your privacy choices], and the same item also appears on the [About] screen. It is shown or hidden depending on your region, as described below.

2.1 Users in Korea

  • On first launch, an [About ads] screen asks whether you agree to personalized ads. You can choose [Agree] or [Don't agree].
  • If you choose [Don't agree], the app requests only non-personalized ads. Gameplay is the same either way.
  • You can change your choice at any time at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Your privacy choices].
  • A child under 14 needs the consent of a legal guardian to agree to personalized ads. If you are under 14, please choose [Don't agree] or make the choice together with your legal guardian.

2.2 Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland

  • On first launch, Google's consent screen appears.
  • You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Your privacy choices].

2.3 Residents of certain US states

Users in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Your privacy choices].

If you opt out, your choice is passed to Google, data processing is restricted, and personalized ads are not shown. You can change this choice at any time in the same menu. The developer does not sell personal information for money.

2.4 Users in other regions

The in-app ad choice item is not shown. You can manage ads in these ways:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's ad settings (the menu location depends on your device and Android version)
  • Google My Ad Center: https://myadcenter.google.com

2.5 Automatic collection tools

The app does not use cookies or tracking tools of its own. The ad SDK collects the information in Section 1.2, and you can refuse personalized ads as described in Sections 2.1 to 2.4. If you do not want the information needed to show ads to be sent at all, please stop using and uninstall the app.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Information on your device: deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or clear its storage data in your device's app info settings. App data is excluded from Android backup, so it is not restored if you reinstall the app.
  • Information sent to Google: kept by Google under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Inquiry emails: deleted from the mailbox and trash without delay once handled. If the law requires us to keep something, we keep only that item for the required period and then delete it.

5. Security measures

As an individual developer, the developer takes the following measures.

  • There is no developer server or developer account system, so the developer does not gather or store users' personal information.
  • Game data is stored in the app's private storage, which Android keeps other apps from accessing, and is excluded from Android backup.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone permissions. The permissions it uses are internet access, network state, vibration, and the advertising ID permissions used by the ad SDK.
  • The developer does not receive payment details.
  • Inquiry emails are read only by the personal information protection officer.
  • Google states that data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • If the developer becomes aware of a personal information breach, the developer will notify affected users as required by law and, where required, report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission or the Korea Internet & Security Agency.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You (or a legal guardian, for a child under 14) can ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of personal information, and can withdraw consent. The only personal information the developer keeps directly is inquiry email, so most rights can be exercised yourself as follows.

  • Delete data on your device: uninstall the app, or clear its storage data in your device's app info settings.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent: follow Section 2.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's ad settings.
  • Play Games: sign-in is optional, and you can sign in at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Play Games]. The app has no sign-out menu. You can delete this game's Play Games data and your profile in Google Play's Play Games profile settings: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
  • Purchase records: see your Google Play purchase history in your Google Account. You can reload your purchase status at [Settings] > [Privacy & account] > [Restore purchase].
  • Other requests: email us at the address in Section 9. We will reply within 10 days of receiving your request. If we cannot finish within that time, we will tell you why and when we expect to finish. We may ask for additional information only as needed to confirm that the request comes from you.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. In Korea, where consent is required to process the personal information of a child under 14, the consent of a legal guardian is required (Section 2.1). If you believe a child's personal information is being processed, please let us know at the email address in Section 9.
