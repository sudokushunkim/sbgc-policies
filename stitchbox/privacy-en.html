Embroidery Box Privacy Policy

  • App Name: Embroidery Box (Google Play Package Name: com.sbgc.stitchbox)
  • Personal Information Controller: Individual Developer Kim Seunghoon (Google Play Developer Name: s.h.)
  • Effective Date: November 3, 2026
  • Last Modified: October 26, 2026

Kim Seunghoon (the "Developer") processes the personal information of users of Embroidery Box (the "App") in accordance with applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the App stores and where, whom it sends information to, and how users can make choices and exercise their rights.

Summary

  • The App has no sign-up, the Developer runs no server of its own, and the App uses no analytics tools.
  • Game records and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to Play Games, the same content is also stored in your Play Games account as a "saved game," so you can continue on another device.
  • When you use Play Games (achievements, saved games), Google Play billing, or AdMob ads, the necessary information is sent to Google LLC. Signing in to Play Games and making purchases are optional.
  • The App shows AdMob ads. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, whether ads are personalized depends on your choice in the consent screen. In all other regions, the App does not request personalized ads (it requests non-personalized ads only).

Your progress continues on other devices signed in to the same account. The App merges this device's save with the account's save and saves again at times such as app start, right after sign-in, when you finish a puzzle, when a purchase is granted, when you leave the App, and when you tap [Save now] in Settings.

1.5 Information the Developer Does Not Collect

On some devices of users who have turned on "Automatically share usage & diagnostics," Google Play collects quality information such as app crashes and app-not-responding events and provides it to the Developer through Google Play Console (Android vitals). Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486

2. Ads and Your Choices

The App shows AdMob ads (interstitial ads, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch).

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: The App shows the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen, and whether ads are personalized depends on your choice. The App does not request ads until the consent process is complete. You can change your choice at any time in [Settings] > [More] > [Ad consent] (this menu appears only in regions where the consent screen is required).
  • All other regions (including South Korea): The App does not request personalized ads and requests non-personalized ads only. There is no menu in the App to turn on personalized ads.
  • Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or the advertising ID for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting. Source: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7676680
  • You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • Information stored on the device: Deleted immediately when you delete the App or clear its storage in the device's app info settings.
  • Android Auto Backup: The App's game save files (the main save and one spare copy) are included in Android Auto Backup. If you have turned on Google backup on your device, they are stored in your own Google account backup and may be restored on a new device or after reinstalling. The install ID, share images, and set-aside progress are not backed up. This backup is kept in your Google account, and the Developer cannot see it. If you turn off backup in your device settings, it is not stored.
  • Play Games saved game: Kept in your Play Games account and not deleted when you delete the App. To delete it, use "Delete individual game data" for this game in your Play Games profile settings on Google Play (section 6). If a device with the App installed and signed in remains, its progress will be uploaded again at the next save, so please first delete the App or clear its data on each device, and then delete the game data. Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
  • Information sent to Google: Kept by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Inquiry emails: Deleted from the mailbox and trash without delay once handled. If the law requires keeping them, the basis and items are kept separately and deleted when the period ends.

5. Security Measures

As an individual developer, the Developer takes the following measures:

  • There is no server or account system of the Developer's own, so the Developer does not gather or keep users' personal information.
  • Game data is stored in the app-specific storage area that Android protects from other apps. Cloud save is stored only in your Play Games account, and Android Auto Backup only in your own Google account.
  • The App does not request location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone permissions. The permissions the App uses are vibration, internet, network state, Google Play billing, advertising ID, Android ad services (advertising ID, ad attribution, topics), keep device awake (WAKE_LOCK), foreground service, and an app-only permission that prevents other apps from receiving the App's internal signals. All except vibration are added by the Google Play billing, ads, and Play Games SDKs and Android libraries. None of them are asked of you separately.
  • The Developer does not receive payment method information.
  • Inquiry emails are read only by the personal information protection officer.
  • Google states that data collected by Play Games Services and the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.
  • If the Developer becomes aware of a personal information breach, the Developer will notify users as required by law and, where necessary, report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission or the Korea Internet & Security Agency.

6. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You (or a legal guardian for a child under 14) may request access to, correction of, deletion of, and suspension of processing of personal information, and may withdraw consent. Since the only personal information the Developer keeps directly is inquiry emails, most rights can be exercised directly as follows:

  • Delete device data: Delete the App or clear its storage in the device's app info settings.
  • Play Games sign-in and cloud save: Signing in is optional. You can sign in from [Settings] > [Cloud save] > [Sign in to Play Games] in the App. While signed in, the same place shows the last save time, and [Save now] saves right away. If the device has a Play Games profile, you may be signed in automatically when the App starts (section 1.3). There is no sign-out menu in the App.
  • Delete Play Games data (achievements, saved game): In the Google Play Store app, go to Profile > Settings > Play Games profile, or visit https://play.google.com/games/profile, and delete this game under "Delete individual game data." Please follow the order in section 3 (delete device data first). Guide: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
  • View achievements: [Settings] > [More] > [Achievements] in the App.
  • Purchase history: Check your Google Play purchase history in your Google account. Purchase status can be loaded again from [Settings] > [More] > [Restore purchases] or [Restore purchases] in the shop screen.
  • Ads: Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland can change their choice in [Settings] > [More] > [Ad consent]. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Other requests: Send them to the email in section 9. The Developer will respond within 10 days of receiving the request; if that is not possible, the Developer will tell you the reason and the expected date. The Developer may ask for additional information to the extent needed to confirm that you are the requester.
