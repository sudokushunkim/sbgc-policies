Village of Pieces Idle Privacy Policy

  • App Name: Village of Pieces Idle (Korean title: 조각 마을 키우기; Google Play Package Name: com.sbgc.piecetown)
  • Personal Information Controller: Individual Developer Kim Seunghoon (Google Play Developer Name: s.h.)
  • Effective Date: November 27, 2026
  • Last Modified: November 23, 2026

Kim Seunghoon (the "Developer") processes the personal information of users of Village of Pieces Idle (the "App") in accordance with applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the App stores and where, whom it sends information to, and how users can make choices and exercise their rights.

(This is the English version of the Korean policy. If the two differ, the Korean version at the same site prevails. The English app title is a working name and may change; the package name will not.)

Summary

  • The App has no sign-up, the Developer runs no server of its own, and the App uses no analytics tools.
  • Game records and settings are stored on your device. The App has no cloud save of its own; with Android Auto Backup (your Google account), your game records and purchase ownership status may be restored when you change devices or reinstall the App (section 3).
  • When you use Play Games (achievements), Google Play billing, or AdMob rewarded ads, the necessary information is sent to Google LLC. Signing in to Play Games, making purchases, and watching ads are all optional.
  • The App shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch by pressing a button. In South Korea, personalized ads are requested only if you are 14 or older and agree to them; in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, it depends on your choice in the consent screen; other regions are asked for personalized ads; and in regions that require a consent screen (such as some US states), it follows your choice in that screen (section 2).

1.4 Information the Developer Does Not Collect

  • The App does not ask for your name, phone number, address, date of birth, or other personal details. The App does not read or send the Play Games player's name. The App has no ranking (leaderboard), so it does not show your name or score to other users.
  • The App does not request location, contacts, photos/storage, camera, microphone, or notification permissions.
  • The App does not use its own crash reporting or analytics tools. However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may send error details to Google when an error occurs in the ad feature; this information is described in section 1.2. Information collected by the Play Games SDK on its own is also described in section 1.2.
  • The Developer does not receive or keep the advertising ID. The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses the advertising ID to show ads (section 1.2).

1.5 Google Play App Quality Information (Android vitals)

On some devices of users who have turned on "Automatically share usage & diagnostics," Google Play collects quality information such as app crashes and app-not-responding events and provides it to the Developer through Google Play Console (Android vitals). This information is collected by Google at the device level, not by the App, and depends on your device settings. The Developer uses it only to fix errors and improve quality.
Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486

2. Ads and Your Choices

The App shows only AdMob rewarded ads that you choose to watch by pressing a button. There are no interstitial or banner ads. If you buy the Village Supporter Pass, the rewarded ad buttons are hidden.

  • South Korea: Personalized ads are requested only if you press "I am 14 or older, I agree" in the App's ad consent screen; if you do not answer, only non-personalized ads are requested. You can reopen and change your choice in [Settings] > [Privacy & ad choices] (개인정보·광고 선택).
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: The App shows the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen, and whether ads are personalized depends on your choice. The App does not request ads until the consent process is complete. You can change your choice at any time in [Settings] > [Privacy & ad choices] (this menu appears only in regions where the consent screen is required).
  • All other regions (regions other than South Korea and those that require a consent screen): The App requests personalized ads.
  • Some US states and other regions that require a consent screen: You can refuse personalized ads in Google's consent screen, and if you choose to refuse, the App does not request personalized ads. You can change your choice in [Settings] > [Privacy & ad choices].
  • In any region, if you delete your advertising ID in your device settings, you will not receive personalized ads.
  • Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or the advertising ID for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting. Source: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7676680
  • You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • Information stored on the device: Deleted immediately when you delete the App or clear its storage in the device's app info settings. When you delete the App, the game progress and purchase records on the device are also removed from the device.
  • Android Auto Backup: Settings, game progress records, the currency ledger, purchase ownership status, and records of granted purchases are included in Android Auto Backup to your Google account. They may be restored from this backup when you move to a new device or reinstall. How long the backup is kept and how it works follow your Google account's backup settings and Google's policies. The per-install purchase check value is excluded from backup, is not restored, and is created anew on a new installation. Purchase status on a restored device is checked again with Google Play, so the same purchase is not granted twice.
  • Non-consumable purchases (Village Supporter Pass) are restored by checking again with Google Play; currency received from consumable products returns only when restored from a backup.
  • Play Games achievements: Kept in your Play Games account and not deleted when you delete the App. To delete them, use "Delete individual game data" for this game in your Play Games profile settings on Google Play (section 6). Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
  • Information sent to Google: Kept by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Inquiry emails: Deleted from the mailbox and trash without delay once handled. If the law requires keeping them, the basis and items are kept separately and deleted when the period ends.

5. Security Measures

As an individual developer, the Developer takes the following measures:

  • There is no server or account system of the Developer's own, so the Developer does not gather or keep users' personal information.
  • Game data is stored in the app-specific storage area that Android protects from other apps.
  • The App does not request location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or notification permissions. The permissions the App uses are vibration, internet, network state, Google Play billing, advertising ID, and Android ad services (advertising ID, ad attribution, topics); these are added by the Google Play billing, ads, and Play Games SDKs and Android libraries. None of them are asked of you separately.
  • The Developer does not receive payment method information.
  • Inquiry emails are read only by the personal information protection officer.
  • Google states that data collected by Play Games Services and the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.
  • If the Developer becomes aware of a personal information breach, the Developer will notify users as required by law and, where necessary, report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission or the Korea Internet & Security Agency.

6. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You (or a legal guardian for a child under 14) may request access to, correction of, deletion of, and suspension of processing of personal information, and may withdraw consent. Since the only personal information the Developer keeps directly is inquiry emails, most rights can be exercised directly as follows:

  • Delete device data: Delete the App or clear its storage in the device's app info settings. Game data backed up to your Google account can be deleted in your Google account and device backup settings.
  • Delete Play Games data (achievements): In the Google Play Store app, go to Profile > Settings > Play Games profile, or visit https://play.google.com/games/profile, and delete this game under "Delete individual game data." Guide: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
  • Purchase history: Check your Google Play purchase history in your Google account. Purchase status can be loaded again with [Settings] > [Restore purchases] (구매 복원) in the App.
  • Ads: In regions that require a consent screen, such as South Korea, the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, you can change your choice in [Settings] > [Privacy & ad choices]. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Other requests: Send them to the email in section 9. The Developer will respond within 10 days of receiving the request; if that is not possible, the Developer will tell you the reason and the expected date. The Developer may ask for additional information to the extent needed to confirm that you are the requester.

7. Children's Personal Information

The App is not directed at children, and the Developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information. In South Korea, to agree to personalized ads you must press the confirmation that you are 14 or older; apart from that, the App has no step that asks for or checks age, so the Developer cannot know a user's age. If you believe the personal information of a child under 14 is being processed, please tell the Developer at the email in section 9. The Developer will delete without delay any information the Developer keeps (inquiry emails) and explain how to delete the information stored on the device and in the Google account (sections 3 and 6).
